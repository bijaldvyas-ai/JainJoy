# JainJoy audit – 4 Nov 2026 (v2.9.4, before the selling release)

Checked as a new customer, a returning customer and the admin on the live site, with phone-size screens and the real Supabase project.

## Customer point of view

| # | Finding | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | A returning customer on the same phone (after logout, or with an expired session) lands on **Sign Up**, not Sign In | Confusing; people try to register again and get "already registered" | Remember on the phone that an account exists; show **Sign In** first from then on |
| C2 | Sign In says "Wrong phone number or password" even when the number was never registered | People do not know they must sign up | Check the number first: "This number is not registered – please sign up" |
| C3 | **No way to recover a forgotten password** | Paying customers get locked out | "Forgot password?" sends a WhatsApp request to the admin; the admin sets a temporary password from the dashboard |
| C4 | No subscription, no trial status | Nothing to sell | 7-day free trial from sign-up, then ₹199 for 1 year by UPI QR; trial days left shown on Today |
| C5 | No privacy policy, terms or refund policy | Needed before taking money, and for trust | Add the three pages (Profile → About) |
| C6 | A customer cannot delete their account | Expected by privacy rules | "Delete my account" in Profile |
| C7 | Nothing makes people invite friends | Growth depends only on manual WhatsApp messages | "Invite friends": personal link; each friend who joins gives 7 extra free days (up to 28) |
| C8 | The app can be installed to the home screen, but nothing tells people | Fewer daily users | "Install JainJoy" button where the phone supports it |
| C9 | Sign Up and Today work well on a 360–375 px phone; menus, Parva rules, festive menu, grocery and WhatsApp sharing all pass the 56 tests | – | Keep |

## Admin point of view

| # | Finding | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Customers list has no plan column (trial, paid, expired) | Admin cannot see who must pay | Plan column: "Trial – 3 days left", "Paid till 4 Nov 2027", "Expired" |
| A2 | No payments list | UPI payments cannot be checked or confirmed | Payments section: customer, UTR / transaction ID, date, **Confirm** / **Reject**; confirming unlocks 1 year |
| A3 | No money summary | No business view | Cards: Paid customers, Revenue, Payments waiting, Trials ending in 2 days |
| A4 | No password reset for a customer | Admin cannot help locked-out customers | **Reset password** button sets a temporary password (shown once to the admin) |
| A5 | No export | Hard to keep records | **Download CSV** of customers and payments |
| A6 | The admin area is labelled "Recipe admin" | Customers/payments are hard to find | Rename to **Admin dashboard**, customers and payments first |

## Security

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Customers cannot change their own status, plan or payments directly (row security, no update policy) – good | Keep; new payment and plan changes go through checked server functions |
| S2 | The paywall runs in the app; the plan dates come from the server and customers cannot change them | Acceptable for this app; noted |
| S3 | A "is this number registered?" check lets anyone test numbers one at a time | Acceptable trade-off asked for by the owner; Supabase rate limits apply |
